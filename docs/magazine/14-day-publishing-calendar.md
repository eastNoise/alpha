# ALPHA Magazine 14-Day Publishing Calendar

Updated: 2026-07-10

## Goal

Launch ALPHA Magazine as a content-first funnel before pushing the app.

The first 14 days should prove whether the audience responds to:

- Cold self-control hooks.
- Saveable carousel posts.
- Short action reels.
- Day 1 as a soft conversion idea.

## Publishing Rules

- Post once per day for 14 days.
- Alternate carousel and reel to test save/share behavior.
- Stories are used for low-friction polls and checklist reposts.
- Do not hard-sell the app before Day 9.
- Every post ends with one action.
- Do not use misogyny, fake wealth, or "alpha male" cliches.

## Metrics To Record Daily

Record after 24 hours and again after 72 hours.

| Metric | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Reach | Whether the hook travels |
| Saves | Whether the content feels useful |
| Shares | Whether the pain point is socially recognizable |
| Profile visits | Whether the brand is interesting enough |
| Follows | Whether users want repeated exposure |
| Story replies | Whether the tone starts conversation |
| CTA taps | Later, whether magazine converts to app |

Minimum weekly review:

```text
What got saved?
What got shared?
What got ignored?
Which line made people reply?
Which CTA felt too salesy?
```

## Review After 14 Days

Decision rules:

1. If saves are high but follows are low:
   - The content is useful, but the brand promise is not clear enough.
   - Strengthen profile/bio and pinned posts.

2. If shares are high:
   - Double down on that pillar.
   - Turn the same hook into a reel within 48 hours.

3. If reels reach but do not convert to profile visits:
   - Make end frame clearer.
   - Add `ALPHA Magazine` and one action.

4. If app CTA performs badly:
   - Delay direct app mentions.
   - Keep pushing `Day 1` as a concept first.

5. If comments/DMs ask for help:
   - Use those exact phrases in the next hooks.
