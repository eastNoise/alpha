# ALPHA Magazine Day 4-7 Production Pack

Updated: 2026-07-10

## Purpose

This pack finishes the first week of ALPHA Magazine production specs.

Day 1-3 attacks the first 10 minutes and phone behavior. Day 4-7 converts that pressure into visible control: bed, body, and weekly review.

The app implementation stays separate. These posts should make people recognize the ALPHA system before the app is pushed hard.

## Shared Rules

- Keep each post about one behavior.
- Make the action small enough to do today.
- Avoid appearance-shaming. The body pillar is about control, energy, and posture.
- Avoid motivational filler like "you can do it" or "believe in yourself."
- Use the same magazine grammar as Day 1-3: hook, mirror, consequence, action, close.
- Do not hard-sell the app yet. The first week is for trust, saves, replies, and identity language.
- If an image uses stock, log the source in `asset-source-register.md`.

### Shot List

1. Phone alarm or dark room cut.
2. Messy bed close-up.
3. Hand pulling blanket flat.
4. Pillow put back in place.
5. Phone placed away from the bed.
6. Final bed shot with hard text overlay.

### Text Overlay Sequence

```text

```

### Caption

```text
침대 정리는 청소가 아니다.
오늘 도망칠 자리를 없애는 행동이다.

일어나면 바로 덮어라.
30초면 된다.

내일 아침 바로 해라.
```

### Visual Direction

Keep cuts fast and physical. The reel should feel like a command, not a room-cleaning tip.

Good movement:

- Blanket snapping into place.
- Phone moved away.
- Feet leaving the room.

Avoid:

- Slow aesthetic cleaning montage.
- Smiling model.
- Soft productivity music mood.

### Alt Text


## First Week Review Checklist

Review these numbers 24 hours after Day 7 and again after 72 hours.

| Signal | Good sign | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saves | Day 1, Day 4, or Day 7 saves lead | Keep carousel as main format |
| Shares | Day 2, Day 5, or Day 6 shares lead | Make more direct behavior attacks |
| Story poll response | Day 4 or Day 6 gets responses | Use more yes/no confession polls |
| Profile visits | Day 7 lifts profile visits | Add pinned "Day 1" guide next |
| Negative replies | Tone called too harsh without action | Soften close, not hook |
| Low completion | Reel drops early before action | Put action by second 3 |

## Week Two Bridge

If the first week gets meaningful saves or replies, Week 2 should widen the same system:

```text
Week 1: 처음 10분 / 침대 / 몸
Week 2: 도파민 / 돈 / 관계 / 하루 닫기
```

Do not jump into app sales only because one post does well. The app CTA should appear after the account has at least one saved Day 1 guide, one dopamine post, and one weekly review post.

Recommended first app bridge:

```text
Day 1은 피드에 저장해라.
Day 2부터는 앱에서 닫아라.
```
